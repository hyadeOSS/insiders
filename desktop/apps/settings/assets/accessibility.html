Vision

format_size
Text size
Makes text and controls larger in apps and Settings.
contrast
Increase contrast
Makes text and edges stand out more against backgrounds.
blur_off
Reduce transparency
Windows, menus and the taskbar become solid instead of frosted glass, which is easier to read.
palette
Colour filter
Grayscale can help with colour blindness or distraction; inverted colours can help with light sensitivity.

Motion

motion_photos_off
Reduce motion
Turns off animations, window effects, wobbly windows, the rolling credits and the live lock-screen wallpaper. Recommended if movement makes you dizzy or you are sensitive to flashing.

Keyboard and navigation

center_focus_strong
Always show keyboard focus
A bright yellow ring around whatever the keyboard is on, everywhere.
link
Underline links
Shows links by more than their colour.

More

hyadeOS labels its buttons for screen readers, so you can use the one built into your computer: Narrator (Windows: Ctrl + Win + Enter), VoiceOver (macOS: Cmd + F5) or Orca (Linux).
