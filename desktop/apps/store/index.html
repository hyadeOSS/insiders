Publish for everyone

“Add to Store” only lists the app on this computer. To get it into the official catalog, send its entry to the hyadeOS team:

  1. Export the entry.
  2. Then either:
    mergeOpen a pull request
    Add it to store-catalog.json in hyadeOSS/store-catalog.     forumJoin the Discord
    Open a ticket and paste the JSON.

Uploaded images stay on this computer. Put icon.png and a screenshots/ folder in your repository so the official catalog can show them.
