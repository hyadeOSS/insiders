Highlights

Snap windows side by side, use several desktops, and keep the Windows shortcuts you know: Win, Win+D, Win+arrows, Win+V.

Press Win+S to find apps, files and settings, or to do a quick calculation. Win+W opens your widgets: weather, today's events, notes and what's playing.

Install apps straight from GitHub. You see the permissions each app asks for before you install it, and apps can update by themselves.

Everyone gets their own account, password and files. There's also a guest account, parental controls and a personal backup.

Pick a live wallpaper, choose an accent color, and follow music, timers and updates in the dynamic island.
